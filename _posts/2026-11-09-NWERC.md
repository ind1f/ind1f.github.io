After failing to qualify last year, our team managed to earn a spot to compete at NWERC this year. It will take place in Karlsruhe, Germany. Yeah, I have never heard of that city either, so it'd be cool to check it out during the trip.

Our goal is to qualify to EUC, as there's not much to achieve below that, and getting a medal is obviously harder. We have been training quite regularly and our recent performances have been decent, but you need to be more than just decent to qualify, especially in a competitive region like NWERC. 

I think NWERC should be the hardest among the 4 regions in Europe? I'm not entirely sure. Like in the sense that there are many strong schools scattered throughout the region. The UK has Oxford and Cambridge consistently getting medals every year. The Netherlands has TU Delft, UvA, Utrecht. Denmark has Aarhus and Copenhagen. So on and so on.

I think it's possible with some luck, but my expectations are not high. Anyways, I'll try to enjoy the contest and not think too much about this. Should be fun.
